Before you submit a clip

  • Footage is only from the official content folder
  • @callofduty tagged on TikTok and Instagram
  • #Ad in the caption
  • Caption is your own words and matches the clip
  • No invented facts, stats, or dates
  • 9:16, audio audible, no watermarks
  • Submitted separately on both platforms

Before you join any campaign

  • Does it pay on verified views, and who verifies them?
  • What is the payout threshold? Some never pay below 100k views.
  • Do you have written rights to clip the creator's footage?
  • Does it ask you to pay to join? Red flag, not a campaign.
  • Does it promise guaranteed income? Nobody can guarantee views.
  • Do you meet the age requirement? If not, involve a trusted adult.
Views are not income until they clear the campaign's threshold. Treat every payout as zero until it is in your account.
