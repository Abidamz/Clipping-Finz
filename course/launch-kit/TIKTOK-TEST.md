# TikTok ads — the honest version

**₦40,000 ≈ $29. TikTok's floor is $50/day at campaign level, $20/day per ad group.**

That means your budget buys **one day**, not a month. Everything below is built around that
reality: one day, one creative, one question answered.

---

## Step 0 — three checks (5 minutes, before any money moves)

| Check | How | If it fails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can your account target Kenya? | Ads Manager → ad group → Targeting → Location → search Kenya | Kenya missing = Turkish ads to Kenya are impossible from this account. Stop. Use creator promos instead (bottom of this file). |
| Can you pay in USD? | Billing → add card → see if it's accepted | Many Nigerian cards are declined. No working card = no campaign. |
| Is your page live and checkout tested? | Send the link to one Kenyan | If checkout is broken, ads just send paid traffic to a dead end. |

Do not skip any of these. The most common failure with small TikTok budgets is a campaign
that runs, spends, and delivers nothing because the account can't target the country.

## Step 1 — the creative (this matters more than every setting)

One video, 15–25 seconds, shot on your phone, vertical. Not a polished ad — TikTok punishes
polish. Show the workbook on a screen and talk over it, or use on-screen text only.

### Creative A — "the arithmetic" (recommended)

| Time | On screen | Text / voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Workbook page with the numbers table | "100,000 views is worth $200. Here's the rest of the maths." |
| 3–10s | Flip through Ch 1.2 and 1.3 | "Rate, minimum, cap, verification. Four numbers, and most beginners never learn them." |
| 10–18s | Show the traps page | "Here's what gets a clip rejected before anyone looks at the edit." |
| 18–24s | Workbook cover | "33 pages. KSh 1,499. No income promises — page one says so." |

### Creative B — "the rejection"

| Time | On screen | Text / voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Text on dark | "Three reasons your clip got rejected." |
| 2–12s | Each reason on screen | "Watermark. Copied caption. Missing disclosure." |
| 12–20s | Four-pass check page | "All three are caught by a 90-second check most people skip." |
| 20–25s | Cover | "It's in the workbook. Link in bio." |

### Creative C — "the honesty"

| Time | On screen | Text / voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4s | You, straight to camera | "I'm not going to tell you clipping makes you a million naira." |
| 4–12s | Numbers page | "Most beginners earn nothing in month one. Here's what it actually pays." |
| 12–20s | Workbook | "So I wrote the version I wanted: the maths, the traps, the steps." |
| 20–25s | Cover | "KSh 1,499. Link in bio." |

**Rules:** no income claims, no bank-balance screenshots, no "are you broke?" framing. Those
get rejected or restricted, and they break the product's own promise.

## Step 2 — campaign settings (paste these)

| Setting | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Objective | **Traffic** | Cheapest data per naira. A purchase objective needs 50 events to learn — you cannot buy that with $29. |
| Campaign budget | **$25 lifetime**, not daily | Hard cap. You cannot be charged more than this. |
| Ad group budget | $25 (single ad group) | One creative, one audience. Splitting $29 across three ad groups starves all three. |
| Location | **Kenya** only | Your market, and where checkout works. |
| Age | 18–45 | Under 18 cannot contract. |
| Gender | All | Do not narrow. |
| Interests | **Broad / none** | Narrow audiences starve a tiny budget. Let the creative do the targeting. |
| Placements | TikTok only, exclude Pangle | Pangle burns small budgets on junk traffic. |
| Bidding | Lowest cost / Maximum delivery | Do not set a bid cap you don't understand. |
| Schedule | 1 day, run it through | Do not touch it mid-flight. |
| Ad | Creative A, B or C | Pick one. Testing three needs three times the budget. |

**Landing:** your Selar link directly. No funnel, no email capture — it is not built yet.

## Step 3 — what to look at, and when to stop

Give it the full day. Then read these three numbers only:

| Metric | Where | What it tells you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Impressions | Campaign report | Did it deliver at all? Under 5,000 = something is blocking delivery. |
| Clicks | Campaign report | Did the creative earn attention? |
| Checkout starts / sales | Selar dashboard | Did anyone want to buy? |

### How to read it

- **No impressions** → delivery problem (audience, placement, or account restriction). Nothing learned about your product. Fix and rerun, or stop.
- **Impressions but cost per click above $0.50** → the first three seconds are not landing. Rewrite the hook, keep everything else.
- **Clicks but no checkout starts** → the page or the price is the problem, not the ad. Do not spend more on ads.
- **One sale or more** → you have proof of demand at $29. That is the only result that justifies more money later.

### What $29 realistically buys

At Kenyan CPMs, roughly **10,000–25,000 impressions** and a few hundred clicks. Expect
**0–1 sales**. This is not a revenue play — it is a question: *does the ad earn clicks, and
does anyone reach checkout?* If you were hoping for 10 sales, that was never possible at
this budget.

## The alternative use of the same ₦40,000

If the goal is **sales**, not learning, this beats TikTok at this size:

| Option | Cost | Why it works better |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 Kenyan creators, ₦10–13k each | ₦40,000 | Their audience already trusts them. A recommendation converts where a cold ad does not. |
| Affiliate deal: 40% to 5 Kenyan creators, ₦0 up front | Free | Selar's affiliate network tracks it. You pay only on sales. |
| 2 creators + ₦14,000 of ads on the best-performing post | ₦40,000 | Promotion first, then amplify what already worked. |

My recommendation stands: **spend ₦0 on ads this week.** Post the video organically, run ten
DMs a day, and offer 40% affiliate commission to five Kenyan creators. When you have one
organic post that clearly outperformed, *then* put ₦40,000 behind that exact creative —
because by then you know it works.
